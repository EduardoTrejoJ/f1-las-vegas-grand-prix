Las Vegas Grand Prix

Experience Formula 1 Under the Vegas Lights!

About the Event

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is an intense Formula 1 race held on the streets of Las Vegas, Nevada. The race takes place at night, with iconic Vegas landmarks as backdrops and all the LED lights you can think of. This event brings together Formula 1 fans from around the world to experience this incredible event in November.

What to Expect

Three Days of Non-Stop Racing Action!

  • Free Practice: Watch as drivers learn the track!
  • Qualifying: Watch drivers compete for pole position!
  • Grand Prix: Experience the main event as the lights turn green and the engines roar!

Event Schedule

Free Practice - Thursday & Friday

Drivers take to the circuit to familiarize themselves with the track and help their engineers develop a race strategy. This happens on Thursday and Friday before race day.

Qualifying - Friday Night

Drivers compete against one another to set their fastest lap time. This determines where they start on the grid for the Grand Prix on Saturday. A faster lap time means a better starting position!

Grand Prix - Saturday Night

The main event! The grand prix takes place Saturday night as drivers compete for a place on the podium and a chance to win!
